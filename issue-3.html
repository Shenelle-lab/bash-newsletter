To the 2026 Crew — Thank You 💜

You Should Be Incredibly Proud.

We hope you've all made your way home, had a chance to dust off and get some well-earned rest after a mammoth few weeks at the Bash.

There's no sugar-coating it — this was one of the toughest Mundi Mundi Bashes we've ever delivered.

This year challenged us in so many ways. Conditions changed, plans changed, and there were plenty of moments that asked more of everyone than we ever expected.

But through all of it, this crew kept going.

You adapted, backed each other, solved problems, kept your sense of humour and continued to make decisions with our Bashers and the wider team front of mind.

Your professionalism, mateship, resilience and willingness to jump in wherever needed are what make this crew genuinely special.

The Bash simply doesn't happen without an unbelievably dedicated bunch of humans (and the odd doggo 🐶) behind it.

Every one of you should be incredibly proud of this one.

Thank you for sticking together, looking after each other and helping us bring the 2026 Mundi Mundi Bash to life. 💜

From the Top

A Special Thank You

The acronym for Outback Music Festival Group is OMFG. This seems pretty appropriate for this year's Mundi Mundi Bash! :) It will certainly be an edition of the Bash that will be remembered for many years to come.

Considering the difficulties and challenges we experienced this year, the general feedback from everyone involved—patrons, artists and others—has been extremely positive.

As organisers, we were judged not so much on what Mother Nature delivered, but on how we responded to and managed the situation. And the response from our management team, crew, volunteers and contractors was first-rate; as an event team we still managed to shine in such adverse conditions.

Situations like the one we experienced have a high potential to damage the festival's brand and reputation. However due to everyone's amazing efforts—where we all went above and beyond—if anything our reputation has actually been enhanced. That's incredible.

We also learned a lot this year from situations we've never dealt with before. This provides us with the opportunity to review things in detail and make necessary changes so we can be as prepared as possible for future challenges that may come our way.

So whilst it was difficult, I think it has in fact probably strengthened the festival.

I wish to reiterate everything outlined in the earlier crew thankyou. You are all amazing, and I am so proud to be supported by everyone involved. You should be so proud of your efforts.

Greg Donovan Head Honcho · Mundi Mundi Bash

Crew Feedback Survey

After such a challenging Bash, your feedback is more important than ever.

There's plenty for us to reflect on from this year, and as we start looking ahead, we want to understand what worked, what didn't, and where we can make things better for our crew.

We'd really appreciate 10–15 minutes of your time to complete our Crew Feedback Survey. Your responses are anonymous unless you choose to identify yourself, so please be open and constructive.
